<p>Drizzle is a modern, minimal weather app that helps you check current conditions and plan ahead.</p>

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Current weather, feels-like temperature, humidity, wind, UV index, and visibility</li>
<li>24-hour forecast with precipitation probability</li>
<li>Seven-day forecast with daily high and low temperatures</li>
<li>Search for cities worldwide and save favourite locations</li>
<li>Compare weather across saved locations</li>
<li>Sunrise and sunset information</li>
<li>Light, dark, and system themes</li>
<li>Temperature and wind-speed unit preferences</li>
<li>Offline access to recently cached weather data</li>
</ul>

<p>Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo. Drizzle does not require an account or an API key.</p>
